Adaptive Ticket Routing through Retrieval-Augmented In-Context Learning from Analyst Behaviour at CERT-EU

Alex Diaz-Santos, Krzysztof Socha

Proceedings of the 32nd ACM SIGKDD Conference on Knowledge Discovery and Data Mining V.2 (KDD ’26) — Jeju Island, Republic of Korea, August 2026

An adaptive ticket routing system that frames routing as retrieval-augmented in-context learning with temporal feedback learning. Rather than training a static model, the system retrieves similar successfully routed tickets as demonstrations and learns continuously from routine analyst behaviour at zero incremental annotation cost. Over six months of production deployment it achieves 86.3% weighted F1, outperforming supervised baselines by 4.5 points and zero-shot LLM inference by 15 points, running entirely on-premises.

Analysis of the Robustness of NMF Algorithms

Alex Díaz, Damian Steele

arXiv preprint arXiv:2106.02213 — June 2021

We examine three non-negative matrix factorization techniques — L2-norm, L1-norm and L2,1-norm — establishing their performance and robustness in real-world applications such as feature selection, while managing computational complexity and sensitivity to noise. Each approach is evaluated on the ORL and YaleB datasets using Relative Reconstruction Error, Average Accuracy and Normalized Mutual Information under a range of simulated noise scenarios.

Analysis of Classifiers Robust to Noisy Labels

Alex Díaz, Damian Steele

arXiv preprint arXiv:2106.00274 — June 2021

We explore contemporary robust classification algorithms for overcoming class-dependent labelling noise: Forward, Importance Re-weighting and T-revision. We demonstrate methods for estimating the transition matrix in order to obtain better classifier performance when working with noisy data, applying deep learning to three datasets and deriving an end-to-end analysis with unknown noise on CIFAR from scratch.
